## Changes to existing entries

- **Sannio**: removed synonyms `Aglianico del Taburno` and `Taburno`. These name separate DOCGs (Aglianico del Taburno DOCG, its own disciplinare and grape rules), not the Sannio DOC itself, even though they sit inside the same Benevento ("Sannio") production area. Folding a sub-appellation into its parent as a synonym was exactly the pattern flagged as a past mistake. Added `Aglianico del Taburno` as its own DOCG entry, parent `Sannio`, with `Taburno` moved there as its synonym instead.
- **Aglianico del Vulture**: removed synonym `Aglianico del Vulture Superiore`. That is a separate DOCG (stricter yield/ageing rules), not a synonym of the DOC. Added it as its own entry, parent `Aglianico del Vulture`.
- **Castel del Monte**: added its three DOCG children (Bombino Nero, Nero di Troia Riserva, Rosso Riserva), elevated from the DOC in 2011; the DOC entry itself is unchanged.
- No renames of any existing entry name; no re-parenting away from what was already in the library.

## Uncertain calls

- **Parent for Foggia-province and inland Puglia DOCs** (Barletta, Cacc'e Mmitte di Lucera, Colline Joniche Tarantine, Gravina, Locorotondo, Martina Franca, Moscato di Trani, Orta Nova, Ostuni, Rosso di Cerignola, San Severo, Tavoliere delle Puglie): parented directly to `Puglia` rather than to the matching IGT (Daunia, Murgia, Tarantino, Valle d'Itria) because I could not confirm each DOC's zone maps exactly onto the informal IGT footprint the way Salento's DOCs clearly do. The IGTs are listed as siblings under Puglia instead. Flagging in case a reviewer with the geographic detail wants to re-nest some of these under Daunia/Murgia/Tarantino/Valle d'Itria.
- **Asprinio** identity: one source (wein.plus) claims Asprinio is DNA-identical to the Greco used in Greco di Tufo; Wikipedia/Wikidata carry "Asprinio bianco" as its own distinct item. Kept it as a separate new grape rather than merging into `Greco`, per the brief's guidance to flag doubt rather than guess.
- **Sciascinoso**: kept as its own grape without an `Olivella` synonym. Campania sources call it "locally called Olivella," but Lazio's IGT Frusinate treats "Olivella Nera" as a distinct variety, so I left the synonym out to avoid a false match instead of guessing.
- **Malvasia Nera di Brindisi / di Lecce**: genetically identical per DNA studies but treated as separate entries in Italy's official national grape registry and in this scope's brief; kept as two distinct grapes as instructed.
- Grape rules limited to the DOCG/DOC level per the brief; skipped grapes for DOC entries with multi-varietal, non-specific disciplinari (e.g. Cilento, Castel San Lorenzo, generic reds/whites) rather than list every minor permitted variety.
