# fr-bsw: Bordeaux and the South West — research notes

- INAO extranet cahiers des charges (extranet.inao.gouv.fr): Bordeaux (06-2025), Bordeaux Supérieur (06-2025), Coteaux du Quercy (2025) — read directly for grape rules.
- vins-bordeaux.fr (CIVB) — appellation family overview, accessed 2026-09-26.
- fr.wikipedia.org, one article per appellation (Bordeaux family and Sud-Ouest appellations), accessed 2026-09-26; each carries the current cahier's grape list and status, cross-checked against INAO where an INAO PDF was found.

## Changes to existing entries
- **Saint-Émilion**: removed "Saint-Émilion Grand Cru" from its synonyms and gave it its own entry (parent Saint-Émilion), per the scope note — it is a separate AOC with its own cahier and stricter yields/ageing, not a synonym.
- **Fronsac**: removed "Canon-Fronsac" from its synonyms and gave it its own entry (parent Bordeaux) — Canon-Fronsac is a distinct AOC (the Côtes de Canon-Fronsac hillside), not a denomination of Fronsac.
- **Blaye**: renamed to "Blaye Côtes de Bordeaux" (`was: "Blaye"`), re-parented from Bordeaux to the "Côtes de Bordeaux" umbrella AOC, since 2009 the family's official structure. Kept "Blaye" and "Côtes de Blaye" as synonyms.
- **Castillon Côtes de Bordeaux**: re-parented from Bordeaux to "Côtes de Bordeaux" for the same reason (it is one of the umbrella's geographic denominations).
- **Barsac**: re-parented from Sauternes to Graves. Barsac is its own commune AOC that overlaps Sauternes' zone; wines may be sold as either "Barsac" or "Sauternes" but Barsac is not administratively subordinate to Sauternes. Flagged as an uncertain call below.
- **Bergerac**: removed "Pécharmant" from its synonyms and gave Pécharmant its own entry — it is a separate AOC (reds only, different grape/yield rules), not a Bergerac denomination.

## Homonyms
- None of the names in this scope collide with a region name I'm aware of in another country (Bordeaux, Cahors, Buzet, etc. are not used as wine-region names elsewhere).

## Uncertain calls
- **Barsac's parent**: see above — re-parented to Graves rather than Sauternes. Both nestings are defensible; a retailer describing hierarchy would likely say "Barsac, Sauternes, Bordeaux" as alternates rather than strict parent-child, but the schema needs one parent, and Graves is the least wrong "nearest enclosing viticultural unit."
- **Lalande-de-Pomerol's parent**: kept as Bordeaux (already in the library), not Pomerol — it adjoins Pomerol but is not contained by it.
- **Saint-Émilion satellites' parent**: I nested Lussac-, Montagne-, Puisseguin- and Saint-Georges-Saint-Émilion under "Saint-Émilion" because that is how retailers describe them ("a Saint-Émilion satellite"), even though legally they are separate, adjoining AOCs rather than sub-appellations of Saint-Émilion proper.
- **Entre-Deux-Mers Haut-Benauge**: treated as a synonym/geographic denomination of Entre-Deux-Mers rather than a separate entry, since it is a white-wine denomination inside the same AOC, not a distinct appellation.
- **Gaillac Premières Côtes**: nested under Gaillac as a child (it is legally its own AOC for a specific dry-white sub-zone, but always described as "a Gaillac appellation").

## Remaining checker warning
- `Saint-Émilion Grand Cru` triggers a "classification word the lookup already strips" warning on its own name, not a synonym. This is unavoidable: "Grand Cru" is part of the real, legally registered AOC name here (unlike Burgundy's generic "Grand Cru" designation), and the entry must be named that to match what's on the label. Left as-is.

## Notes on grapes
- All South West natives named in the scope note (Petit Manseng, Gros Manseng, Petit Courbu, Arrufiac, Mauzac, Baroque, Malbec/Côt, Fer Servadou, Duras) were already present in `grapes.yaml`; only Camaralet, Lauzet, Ondenc, Raffiat de Moncade, Courbu Noir and Mérille were missing and are added in `fr-bsw.grapes.yaml`.
- Left Saint-Côme, Mouyssaguès and Négret de Banhars (accessory grapes of Entraygues-Le Fel) out of both the grapes file and the region's `grapes:` list — they are minor accessory varieties, not principal ones, per the brief's rule.
- Did not add "Hermitage" anywhere as a grape synonym.
