## Changes to existing entries
- `Alentejo` (DOC): removed synonyms "Alentejano", "Vinho Regional Alentejano", "Regional Alentejano". These name the separate IGP Alentejano (broader zone, looser rules), not the DOC; folding them in as DOC synonyms would have made the IGP's own entry impossible (a synonym may not equal another entry's name) and mixed two different appellations. Added grapes (mandatory reds ≥75%, authorised whites) from CVRA's own DOC page.
- `Península de Setúbal` (VR): removed synonyms "Setúbal", "Moscatel de Setúbal". These name the separate DOC Setúbal (the fortified Moscatel appellation centred on Azeitão), which is a child DOC, not a synonym of the peninsula-wide VR/IGP. Added new child entry `Setúbal` (DOC) to carry them instead.
- `Açores` (VR): removed synonym "Pico". Pico is one of the three Azorean DOCs (an island-specific appellation), not a synonym for the whole archipelago-wide VR/IGP. Added `Pico` as its own child DOC entry instead.

## Homonyms
- None of Alenquer, Arruda, Bucelas, Carcavelos, Colares, Lourinhã, Óbidos, Torres Vedras, Tomar, Coruche, Almeirim, Cartaxo, Santarém, Chamusca, Borba, Évora, Moura, Portalegre, Redondo, Reguengos, Vidigueira, Palmela, Setúbal, Lagos, Lagoa, Portimão, Tavira, Madeira, Madeirense, Biscoitos, Graciosa or Pico are known to name a wine region in another country in this library.

## Uncertain calls
- `Vital`: several glossaries (wein.plus) list "Malvasia Corada" and even "Malvasia Fina" among its synonyms, but Wikipedia's grape page treats Vital as its own variety (a 2021 DNA study makes it a natural cross of Malvasia Fina × Rabo de Ovelha, i.e. a distinct offspring, not the same plant). Added Vital as a new, separate grape and left "Malvasia Corada" out of its synonyms to avoid conflating it with Malvasia Fina; flagging here instead of guessing.
- `Negra Mole` vs `Tinta Negra`/Negramoll (already merged by es-s1): some retailer copy treats Algarve's Negra Mole and Madeira's Tinta Negra as the same plant under regional names; Wikipedia's "Tinta Negra Mole" article covers both together. Kept them as two separate grapes (Negra Mole new here, Negramoll/Tinta Negra already in grapes.yaml) because the scope lists them separately and ampelographic sources are inconsistent; flagging for the merge step to check VIVC.
- `Madeirense` DOC: treated as a sibling of `Madeira`, not a child, since it is a separate, later appellation (covers Madeira + Porto Santo, mostly still/non-fortified wine) rather than a sub-zone of the fortified Madeira DOC. Confirm against IVV's own hierarchy if this is reviewed.
- Algarve's four DOCs (Lagos, Lagoa, Portimão, Tavira): detailed casta rules were only found in full for Lagos; the other three are given the region's shared traditional varieties (Castelão, Negra Mole, Arinto, Síria) per the Comissão de Vinhos do Algarve's general varieties page. If a reviewer has the individual caderno de especificações for Lagoa/Portimão/Tavira, the grape lists may need refining per DOC.
- Tejo and Alentejo sub-regions: not given their own `grapes:` list since their rules mirror the parent DOC's list exactly (confirmed for Tejo: "autorizadas as mesmas castas em todas as designações"); grapes are on the parent entry only.
