# cl: Chile

**Country:** CL. **Slice:** all of Chile, one agent.

**You own:** every existing CL entry and everything under it, following
the Denominación de Origen system (Decreto 464 and its amendments):
- **Regions:** Atacama, Coquimbo, Aconcagua, Central Valley, Southern
  Chile (Región del Sur), Austral. Keep the existing English names
  (`Central Valley`, `Southern Chile`) and carry the Spanish as synonyms.
- **Subregions:** Copiapó, Huasco; Elqui, Limarí, Choapa; Aconcagua
  Valley, Casablanca, San Antonio, Marga-Marga; Maipo, Rapel, Curicó,
  Maule; Itata, Bío-Bío, Malleco; Cautín, Osorno. Nest the Aconcagua
  region vs Aconcagua Valley subregion carefully, and say how in the `.md`.
- **Zones:** Leyda (under San Antonio), Cachapoal and Colchagua (under
  Rapel), Teno and Lontué (under Curicó), Claro, Loncomilla and Tutuvén
  (under Maule), and the rest.
- **Areas that labels use:** e.g. Puente Alto, Pirque, Buin, Isla de
  Maipo, Talagante; Apalta, Santa Cruz, Marchigüe, Lolol, Peralillo,
  Nancagua, Chimbarongo; Peumo, Requínoa, Rengo; Sagrada Familia,
  Molina; Cauquenes; Quillón, Portezuelo, Coelemu, Guarilihue. Aim for
  25–40; prefer those on export labels.
- **The 2011 complementary terms** Costa, Entre Cordilleras and Andes are
  qualifiers, not regions ("Maipo Andes"). Record them in the `.md`; don't
  add them as entries. Secano Interior is a legal designation for País and
  Cinsault; decide whether it's a region and say why.

**Existing entries to fix:** check the synonyms of each existing entry
for folded-in zones or areas (`Maipo Valley` carries "Alto Maipo", which
is an informal label term; decide and say why).

**Homonyms, record in the `.md`:** Central Valley (CL/US), Patagonia
(CL/AR).

**Not yours:** nothing else; Chile is one slice.

**Official register:** SAG's Denominación de Origen list and Decreto 464
(bcn.cl). Count regions, subregions, zones and areas against it.

**`grapes:` field:** none. Chilean DOs have no grape rules.

**Grapes file:** Chilean names and synonyms missing from `grapes.yaml`.
Run `context --grape` on each first; add only missing grapes and missing
synonyms: Carménère (Grande Vidure), País (Listán Prieto; Mission and
Criolla Chica are synonyms: check VIVC), Moscatel de Alejandría as a
Chilean label name, Pedro Jiménez (Chile's; a different variety from
