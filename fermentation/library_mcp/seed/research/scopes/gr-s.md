# gr-s: Greece, south and Aegean

**Country:** GR. **Slice:** Peloponnese, Crete, the Cyclades, the
Dodecanese and the North Aegean islands. Greece is split across two
agents (gr-n, gr-s).

**You own:** the existing `Peloponnese`, `Nemea`, `Mantinia`, `Patras`,
`Santorini`, `Crete` and `Samos` entries and everything under them, plus
every PDO and PGI in these regions:
- **PDOs:** e.g. Nemea, Mantinia, Patras, Mavrodaphne of Patras, Muscat
  of Patras, Muscat of Rio Patras, Monemvasia-Malvasia, Santorini, Paros,
  Malvasia Paros, Rhodes, Muscat of Rhodes, Samos, Lemnos, Muscat of
  Lemnos, Archanes, Dafnes, Peza, Sitia, Chandakas-Candia, Malvasia
  Chandakas-Candia, Malvasia Sitia. Count against the register.
- **PGIs:** the regional ones (Peloponnese, Crete, South Aegean, North
  Aegean) and the local ones under them (e.g. Corinth, Arcadia,
  Laconia, Messinia, Achaia, Ilia, Heraklion, Lasithi, Chania,
  Rethymno, Cyclades, Ikaria, Lesbos, Chios). Classification `PGI`,
  nested regional > local.

Use the Latin spelling labels use, and add transliteration variants as
synonyms. Vinsanto (Santorini) is a style, not a region.

**Not yours:** gr-n: Macedonia, Thrace, Epirus, Thessaly, Central
Greece (Attica, Evia) and the Ionian Islands (Cephalonia's PDOs
included).

**Official register:** eAmbrosia (Greece's PDOs and PGIs) first, then
the Greek Ministry of Rural Development and newwinesofgreece.com. Count
PDOs and PGIs against eAmbrosia.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every PDO:** the varieties its rules name.
For example, Nemea: Agiorgitiko; Santorini: Assyrtiko, Athiri, Aidani.

**Grapes file:** natives and local names missing from `grapes.yaml`.
Run `context --grape` on each first; add only missing grapes and
missing synonyms: Agiorgitiko (St. George), Moschofilero, Roditis,
Mavrodaphne, Assyrtiko, Aidani, Athiri, Mandilaria, Mavrotragano,
Vidiano, Liatiko, Kotsifali, Vilana, Thrapsathiri, Dafni, Plyto,
Kydonitsa, Monemvasia, Limnio, Fokiano, Muscat of Samos / Moschato
(check which Muscat each PDO means: Samos is Muscat Blanc à Petits
Grains, Lemnos is Muscat of Alexandria). Add transliteration variants
as synonyms. gr-n owns Xinomavro, Malagousia, Savatiano, Robola and
the northern grapes.
