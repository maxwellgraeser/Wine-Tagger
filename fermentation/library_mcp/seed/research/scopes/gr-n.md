# gr-n: Greece, north, centre and Ionian

**Country:** GR. **Slice:** Macedonia, Thrace, Epirus, Thessaly, Central
Greece (Sterea Ellada, Attica, Evia) and the Ionian Islands. Greece is
split across two agents (gr-n, gr-s).

**You own:** the existing `Macedonia`, `Naoussa`, `Amyndeon`, `Drama`,
`Goumenissa`, `Rapsani`, `Attica` and `Cephalonia` entries and everything
under them, plus every PDO and PGI in these regions:
- **PDOs:** e.g. Naoussa, Amyndeon, Goumenissa, Slopes of Meliton (Côtes
  de Meliton), Rapsani, Anchialos, Messenikola, Zitsa, Robola of
  Cephalonia, Mavrodaphne of Cephalonia, Muscat of Cephalonia. Count
  against the register.
- **PGIs:** the regional ones (Macedonia, Thrace, Epirus, Thessaly,
  Central Greece, Attica, Ionian Islands) and the local ones under them
  (e.g. Drama, Kavala, Pangeon, Halkidiki, Mount Athos, Epanomi,
  Thessaloniki, Florina, Metsovo, Ioannina, Tyrnavos, Meteora, Evia,
  Markopoulo, Pallini, Corfu, Zakynthos, Lefkada). Classification `PGI`,
  nested regional > local.

Use the Latin spelling labels use, and add transliteration variants as
synonyms (Amyndeon / Amyntaio, Naoussa / Náoussa).

**Homonyms, record in the `.md`:** Macedonia (GR) vs North Macedonia
(Wave 3); Thrace (GR/TR/BG). Keep the GR entries named "Macedonia" and
"Thrace".

**Not yours:** gr-s: Peloponnese, Crete, the Cyclades, the Dodecanese
and the North Aegean islands (Samos, Lemnos, Lesbos, Ikaria, Chios).

**Official register:** eAmbrosia (Greece's PDOs and PGIs) first, then the
Greek Ministry of Rural Development and newwinesofgreece.com. Count PDOs
and PGIs against eAmbrosia.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every PDO:** the varieties its rules name.
For example, Naoussa: Xinomavro; Goumenissa: Xinomavro, Negoska.

Run `context --grape` on each first; add only missing grapes and
missing synonyms: Xinomavro, Negoska, Limniona, Krasato, Stavroto,
Mavroudi, Vlachiko, Bekari, Debina, Malagousia, Savatiano, Roditis is

Batiki, Asproudes. Add transliteration variants as synonyms
