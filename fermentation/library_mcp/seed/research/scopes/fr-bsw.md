# fr-bsw: Bordeaux and the South West

**Country:** FR. **Slice:** Bordeaux and the South West. France is split
across four agents (fr-bsw, fr-bjs, fr-rpl, fr-lac).

**You own:** the existing `Bordeaux` and `South West France` entries and
everything under them, plus:
- **Bordeaux, every AOC:**
  - the Médoc, Graves and Right Bank appellations;
  - the satellites: Montagne-, Lussac-, Puisseguin- and
    Saint-Georges-Saint-Émilion;
  - Francs, Cadillac, Blaye and Castillon Côtes de Bordeaux;
  - the sweet-wine AOCs: Sauternes, Barsac, Loupiac, Cadillac,
    Sainte-Croix-du-Mont, Cérons;
  - Graves Supérieures, Graves de Vayres, Sainte-Foy, Bordeaux Clairet,
    Crémant de Bordeaux, and the rest.

  Saint-Émilion Grand Cru is its own AOC.
- **The South West:** Bergerac and its AOCs (Monbazillac, Pécharmant,
  Saussignac, Montravel...), Côtes de Duras, Côtes du Marmandais, Buzet,
  Brulhois, Cahors, Gaillac, Fronton, Marcillac, Madiran, Pacherenc du
  Vic-Bilh, Saint-Mont, Tursan, Jurançon, Béarn, Irouléguy, Entraygues-Le
  Fel, Estaing, Côtes de Millau, and the rest.
- **The IGPs of these areas:** Côtes de Gascogne, Comté Tolosan, Côtes du
  Lot, Périgord, Atlantique, Agenais, and so on.

**Not yours:**
- fr-bjs: Burgundy, Beaujolais, Jura, Savoie
- fr-rpl: Rhône, Provence, Corsica, Languedoc-Roussillon
- fr-lac: Loire, Alsace, Champagne, national IGPs, Vin de France

**Official register:** INAO (inao.gouv.fr: the appellation list and each
cahier des charges) and eAmbrosia. Count AOCs and IGPs against them. The
existing convention is `classification: AOC` (not AOP) and `IGP`.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every AOC:** the principal varieties its
cahier names. For example, Cahors: Malbec, Merlot, Tannat; Jurançon: Petit
Manseng, Gros Manseng.

**Grapes file:** South West natives and local names missing from
`grapes.yaml`. Check Petit Manseng, Gros Manseng, Petit Courbu, Arrufiac,
Camaralet, Lauzet, Len de l'El, Mauzac, Ondenc, Baroque, Raffiat de
Moncade, Courbu Noir and Mérille, and local names such as Côt / Auxerrois
for Malbec. Several are already present.

Previous names matter as synonyms: "Premières Côtes de Bordeaux", "Côtes
de Castillon", "Bordeaux Côtes de Francs".
