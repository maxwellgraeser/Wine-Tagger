# uy-ca: Uruguay and Canada

**Country:** UY, CA. **Slice:** two countries, one agent. (The plan's
SMALL agent is split in two: ch, and uy-ca.)

**You own:** every existing UY and CA entry and everything under it.
- **Uruguay (UY):** the six regions of the 2019 INAVI zoning (Costa
  Oceánica, Metropolitana, Sur Oeste, Centro Sur, Litoral Norte, Noreste; check the names)
  and the departments labels use (Canelones, Montevideo, Maldonado,
  Colonia, San José, Rivera, Salto, Paysandú, Florida, Durazno…), plus the
  label areas (Garzón, Las Violetas, Progreso, Juanicó, Carmelo,
  Atlántida…). The existing `Canelones` and `Maldonado` stay as departments.
- **Canada (CA):** VQA Ontario: the three Designated Viticultural Areas
  (Niagara Peninsula, Lake Erie North Shore, Prince Edward County) and
  Niagara's two regional and ten sub-appellations (Niagara Escarpment,
  Niagara-on-the-Lake; Short Hills Bench, Twenty Mile Bench, Beamsville
  Bench, Vinemount Ridge, Creek Shores, Lincoln Lakeshore, Four Mile Creek,
  Niagara River, St. David's Bench, Niagara Lakeshore). Add Ontario's
  newer DVAs if the VQA list has them. BC VQA: the GIs (Okanagan Valley,
  Similkameen Valley, Fraser Valley, Vancouver Island, Gulf Islands,
  Thompson Valley, Shuswap, Lillooet, Kootenays) and the Okanagan
  sub-GIs (Golden Mile Bench, Naramata Bench, Skaha Bench, Okanagan
  Falls, Summerland…). Nova Scotia (Tidal Bay is a wine style: note it,
  don't add it), Québec (IGP Vin du Québec).
  Classification: `DVA`, `Sub-appellation`, `GI`, `Sub-GI` as the rules
  name them.

Aim for 50–65 entries.

**Homonyms, record in the `.md`:** Niagara (CA / the US Niagara Escarpment
AVA: both exist), Lake Erie (US AVA / Lake Erie North Shore), Colonia.

**Not yours:** nothing else.

**Official register:** INAVI (inavi.com.uy) for Uruguay; VQA Ontario's
appellation list and the BC Wine Authority's GI list for Canada.

**`grapes:` field:** none. Neither country has grape rules per area.

**Grapes file:** names missing from `grapes.yaml`. Run `context --grape`
on each first; add only missing grapes and missing synonyms. Tannat
(Harriague), Vidal, Baco Noir and Marquette exist: check synonyms.
Candidates: Maréchal Foch, L'Acadie Blanc, Seyval Blanc, Frontenac,
Marselan if missing. Vidal Blanc is a synonym of Vidal if missing.
