# us-pnw: Pacific Northwest

**Country:** US. **Slice:** Oregon, Washington, Idaho. The US is split
across three agents (us-ca, us-pnw, us-rest).

**You own:**
- The existing `Oregon`, `Washington` and `Idaho` entries. Add any that are
  missing as state-level entries, following the existing US convention.
- Everything under them: every AVA in the three states, nested properly.
  For example:
  - Willamette Valley > its sub-AVAs: Dundee Hills, Eola-Amity Hills,
    Yamhill-Carlton, Ribbon Ridge, Chehalem Mountains, McMinnville, Van
    Duzer Corridor, Laurelwood District, Tualatin Hills, Lower Long Tom,
    Mount Pisgah Polk County.
  - Southern Oregon > Rogue Valley / Applegate Valley / Umpqua Valley.
  - Columbia Valley > Yakima Valley > Red Mountain.
- AVAs that straddle these states: Columbia Valley, Walla Walla Valley,
  Columbia Gorge, Snake River Valley, The Rocks District of
  Milton-Freewater. Give each one parent (the state labels most often
  attribute it to, or the larger containing AVA) and record the straddle in
  the `.md`.

**Not yours:** California (us-ca) and every other state (us-rest).

**Official register:** TTB's list of established AVAs (ttb.gov) and 27 CFR
part 9 (ecfr.gov). Count your AVAs against it, including recently
established ones.

**`grapes:` field:** none. AVAs have no grape rules.

**Grapes file:** only PNW-specific grape names or synonyms missing from
`grapes.yaml`. There are probably few or none; say so in the `.md` if none.
