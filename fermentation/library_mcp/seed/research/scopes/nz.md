# nz: New Zealand

**Country:** NZ. **Slice:** all of New Zealand, one agent.

**You own:** every existing NZ entry and everything under it:
- **All registered GIs** under the Geographical Indications (Wine and
  Spirits) Registration Act (about 20): Northland, Auckland, Matakana,
  Kumeu, Waiheke Island, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Central Hawke's Bay,
  Wairarapa, Martinborough, Gladstone, Wellington, Nelson, Marlborough,
  North Canterbury, Waipara Valley, Canterbury, Waitaki Valley, Central
  Otago, and any registered since. Nest them (Waiheke Island and
  Matakana under Auckland, Martinborough under Wairarapa, Waipara Valley
  under North Canterbury under Canterbury). Classification `GI`.
- **Label subregions that are not GIs** but lead labels: Marlborough's
  Wairau Valley, Southern Valleys, Awatere Valley; Central Otago's
  Bannockburn, Gibbston, Bendigo, Cromwell, Lowburn, Pisa, Alexandra,
  Wanaka; Hawke's Bay's Gimblett Gravels (existing), Bridge Pa Triangle;
  Nelson's Moutere Hills, Waimea Plains. No classification; say in each
  entry's `source` note which body defines it.

**Not yours:** nothing else; NZ is one slice.

**Official register:** IPONZ's register of wine GIs (iponz.govt.nz) and
nzwine.com. Count GIs against the IPONZ register.

**`grapes:` field:** none. NZ GIs have no grape rules.

**Grapes file:** only NZ-specific grape names or synonyms missing from
`grapes.yaml`. There are probably few or none; say so in the `.md` if
none.
