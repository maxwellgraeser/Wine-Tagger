# au-w: Australia, west (SA, WA, Tasmania)

**Country:** AU. **Slice:** South Australia, Western Australia,
Tasmania, Northern Territory. Australia is split across two agents
(au-w, au-e).

**You own:** the existing `South Australia`, `Western Australia` and
`Tasmania` entries and everything under them, following the Wine
Australia Register of Protected GIs:
- **Zones:** SA: Adelaide (super zone), Barossa, Fleurieu, Limestone
  Coast, Lower Murray, Mount Lofty Ranges, The Peninsulas, Far North; WA:
  Greater Perth, South West Australia, Central Western Australia, Eastern
  Plains Inland and North of Western Australia, West Australian South
  East Coastal. Classification `Zone`.
- **Regions:** every GI region in these zones (Barossa Valley, Eden
  Valley, Clare Valley, McLaren Vale, Adelaide Hills, Langhorne Creek,
  Currency Creek, Kangaroo Island, Southern Fleurieu, Coonawarra,
  Padthaway, Wrattonbully, Mount Benson, Robe, Mount Gambier, Riverland,
  Adelaide Plains, Southern Flinders Ranges; Margaret River, Great
  Southern, Pemberton, Manjimup, Geographe, Blackwood Valley, Peel, Perth
  Hills, Swan District). Classification `GI`.
- **Subregions:** High Eden; Lenswood, Piccadilly Valley; Albany,
  Denmark, Frankland River, Mount Barker, Porongurup; Swan Valley.
  Classification `Subregion`. Tasmania has no GI regions; add its label
  subregions (Tamar Valley, Coal River Valley, Huon Valley, Derwent
  Valley, Pipers River, East Coast) without a classification and say so.
- Label-used unofficial names such as Clare's Polish Hill River and
  Watervale, or Barossa's Ebenezer, go in only as children without a
  classification; aim for at most 10.

The existing `South Australia` entry is classified `Zone`. States are GIs
of their own on the register; decide whether `State` fits better and apply
the same to Western Australia and Tasmania.

**Not yours:** au-e: Victoria, New South Wales, Queensland, the ACT and
the multi-state GIs (South Eastern Australia, and Murray Darling / Swan
Hill, which straddle Victoria and NSW).

**Official register:** Wine Australia's Register of Protected GIs and
Other Terms (wineaustralia.com). Count zones, regions and subregions
against it.

**`grapes:` field:** none. Australian GIs have no grape rules.

**Grapes file:** only Australian names or synonyms missing from
`grapes.yaml` that belong to your states (e.g. Mataro for Mourvèdre).
There are probably few; say so in the `.md`. Avoid place names as grape
synonyms ("Hermitage" for Shiraz is a known trap).
