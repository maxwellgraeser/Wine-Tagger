  Central Anatolia, Eastern Anatolia, Mediterranean, Black Sea) and the

  Tokat…). Turkey has few legal GIs: say in the `.md` which are legal
  (Türk Patent) and which are informal.
- **Lebanon (LB):** Bekaa Valley (West Bekaa, Zahlé), Mount Lebanon,
  Batroun, Jezzine, and the areas labels use. No formal register: say what
  you used.
