# fr-lac: Loire, Alsace, Champagne, and national-level France

**Country:** FR. **Slice:** Loire, Alsace, Champagne, and national-level
France. France is split across four agents (fr-bsw, fr-bjs, fr-rpl,
fr-lac).

**You own:**
- The existing `Loire Valley`, `Alsace`, `Champagne` and `Vin de France`
  entries and everything under them.
- **Any existing FR entry that is not under** Bordeaux, South West France,
  Burgundy, Beaujolais, Jura, Savoie, Rhône Valley, Provence, Corsica,
  Languedoc-Roussillon or Pays d'Oc. Check `regions.yaml`.
- **Pays Nantais:** Muscadet and its sub-AOCs and crus communaux; Gros
  Plant.
- **Anjou-Saumur:** Anjou, Anjou-Villages, Savennières and its two
  sub-AOCs, Coteaux du Layon and its villages, Quarts de Chaume,
  Bonnezeaux, Coteaux de l'Aubance, Saumur, Saumur-Champigny, Crémant de
  Loire, Rosé d'Anjou, Cabernet d'Anjou...
- **Touraine:** Touraine and its named areas, Chinon, Bourgueil,
  Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil, Vouvray, Montlouis-sur-Loire, Cheverny,
  Cour-Cheverny, Jasnières, Valençay...
- **Centre-Loire:** Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé, Pouilly-sur-Loire,
  Menetou-Salon, Quincy, Reuilly, Coteaux du Giennois, Châteaumeillant.
- **Auvergne and the upper Loire:** Côte Roannaise, Côtes du Forez, Côtes
  d'Auvergne, Saint-Pourçain.
- **Alsace:** the Alsace AOC, Crémant d'Alsace, and **all 51 Alsace Grand
  Cru AOCs as their own entries** under Alsace Grand Cru (Schlossberg,
  Rangen, Hengst, Brand...).
- **Champagne:**
  - Champagne, Coteaux Champenois, Rosé des Riceys.
  - As informal children with no classification: the five sub-areas
    (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte de
    Sézanne, Côte des Bar) and the 17 Grand Cru villages (Aÿ, Bouzy,
    Ambonnay, Cramant, Avize, Le Mesnil-sur-Oger...).
- **Lorraine:** Côtes de Toul and the Moselle AOC. Moselle shares its name
  with the German Mosel; list it as a homonym.
- **IGPs:** IGP Val de Loire and any IGPs not tied to another slice's
  area. Vin de France stays national.

**Not yours:**
- fr-bsw: Bordeaux, South West
- fr-bjs: Burgundy, Beaujolais, Jura, Savoie
- fr-rpl: Rhône, Provence, Corsica, Languedoc-Roussillon

**Official register:** INAO (inao.gouv.fr) and eAmbrosia. Count against
them. The existing convention is `classification: AOC` and `IGP`.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every AOC:** the principal varieties its
cahier names. For example:
- Sancerre: Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir
- Vouvray: Chenin Blanc
- Muscadet: Melon de Bourgogne
- Alsace Grand Cru: Riesling, Gewürztraminer, Pinot Gris, Muscat
- Champagne: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Pinot Meunier

**Grapes file:** local grapes and names missing from `grapes.yaml`. Check
Romorantin, Pineau d'Aunis, Grolleau, Menu Pineau / Orbois, Chasselas,
Sylvaner, Klevener de Heiligenstein, Pinot Auxerrois, Tokay (the old
Alsace name for Pinot Gris), Gros Plant (= Folle Blanche), Arbane, Petit
Meslier, Fié Gris, and Breton (= Cabernet Franc).
