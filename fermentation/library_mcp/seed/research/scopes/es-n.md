# es-n: Northern Spain

**Country:** ES. **Slice:** Northern Spain. Spain is split across two
agents (es-n, es-s).

**You own:** the existing ES entries in these autonomous communities and
everything under them, plus every DOCa, DOQ, DO, Vino de Pago (VP), Vino de
Calidad (VC) and IGP / Vino de la Tierra in them:
- **Galicia:** Rías Baixas with its sub-zones (Val do Salnés, Condado do
  Tea, O Rosal, Soutomaior, Ribeira do Ulla), Ribeiro, Ribeira Sacra,
  Valdeorras, Monterrei.
- **Asturias** and **Cantabria.**
- **País Vasco:** the three Txakoli DOs.
- **Navarra.**
- **La Rioja:** Rioja DOCa with Rioja Alta, Rioja Alavesa and Rioja
  Oriental.
- **Aragón:** Somontano, Campo de Borja, Calatayud, Cariñena.
- **Catalonia:** Priorat (DOQ), Montsant, Penedès, Clàssic Penedès,
  Catalunya, Costers del Segre, Terra Alta, Empordà, Conca de Barberà,
  Alella, Tarragona, Pla de Bages.
- **Castilla y León:** Ribera del Duero, Rueda, Toro, Bierzo, Cigales,
  Arlanza, Arribes, Tierra de León, Tierra del Vino de Zamora, Valles de
  Benavente, Sierra de Salamanca, Valtiendas, VT Castilla y León.
- **Cava:** cross-regional. Give it no parent, or Catalonia per the
  existing convention, and note the straddle.

The existing entry "La Rioja" is a synonym of Rioja. Argentina also has a
La Rioja wine region. That is allowed now; list it under homonyms.

**Not yours:** Castilla-La Mancha, Madrid, Valencia, Murcia, Andalucía,
Extremadura, the Balearics and the Canaries (es-s).

**Official register:** MAPA's list of DOPs/IGPs (mapa.gob.es) and
eAmbrosia. Count against them. The existing convention is
`classification: DOCa` / `DOQ` / `DO`; use `VP`, `VC` and `IGP` for the
others.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every DOCa/DOQ/DO/VP:** the principal
varieties its pliego de condiciones names. For example, Rioja: Tempranillo,
Garnacha, Graciano, Mazuelo, Viura; Rías Baixas: Albariño; Rueda:
Verdejo.

**Grapes file:** northern Spanish natives and local names missing from
`grapes.yaml`. Several exist already; add only missing grapes and missing
synonyms.
- Tempranillo aliases: Tinto Fino, Tinta del País, Tinta de Toro, Ull de
  Llebre.
- Garnacha forms: Garnatxa (the tagger asked for it and got nothing),
  Garnacha Peluda, Garnacha Tintorera (= Alicante Bouschet).
- Other reds: Graciano, Mazuelo / Samsó (= Carignan), Mencía, Sousón,
  Brancellao, Merenzao (= Trousseau), Espadeiro, Sumoll, Trepat,
  Hondarrabi Beltza, Prieto Picudo, Juan García, Rufete, Bruñal, Moristel,
  Maturana.
- Whites: Godello, Treixadura, Loureira, Caíño, Verdejo, Viura / Macabeo,
  Xarel·lo, Parellada, Garnacha Blanca, Picapoll, Hondarrabi Zuri, Doña
  Blanca, Tempranillo Blanco.
