# it-n: Northern Italy

**Country:** IT. **Slice:** Northern Italy. Italy is split across three
agents (it-n, it-c, it-s).

**You own:** the existing entries for Piedmont, Valle d'Aosta, Liguria,
Lombardy, Trentino-Alto Adige, Veneto, Friuli-Venezia Giulia and
Emilia-Romagna, and everything under them: every DOCG, DOC and IGT in
these eight regions. Interregional denominations whose main area is here
are also yours: delle Venezie IGT/DOC, and Prosecco DOC (parent Veneto;
note the straddle).
- **Piedmont:** Barolo, Barbaresco, Roero, Gavi, the Asti / Moscato d'Asti
  / Barbera d'Asti group, Nizza, Langhe, Dogliani, Ghemme, Gattinara, the
  Alto Piemonte DOCs...
- **Veneto:** Amarone / Recioto / Valpolicella / Valpolicella Ripasso,
  Soave, Bardolino, Lugana (straddles Lombardy), Conegliano Valdobbiadene
  Prosecco, Asolo Prosecco...
- **Lombardy:** Franciacorta, Valtellina Superiore, Sforzato di
  Valtellina, Oltrepò Pavese...
- **Trentino-Alto Adige:** Alto Adige / Südtirol and its sub-zones,
  Trentino, Trento DOC, Teroldego Rotaliano...
- **Friuli-Venezia Giulia:** Collio, Friuli Colli Orientali, Ramandolo,
  Carso, Friuli Grave...
- **Emilia-Romagna:** the Lambrusco DOCs, Romagna Albana, Colli
  Bolognesi...

**Names:**
- The canonical name follows the existing entries (Piedmont, Lombardy);
  the Italian form is a synonym (Piemonte, Lombardia).
- German names in Alto Adige are synonyms (Südtirol, Kalterersee).
- Collio (IT) and Goriška Brda (SI) are one cross-border zone under two
  names. List it under homonyms.
- Do NOT add Barolo MGAs or other menzioni geografiche aggiuntive as
  entries.

**Not yours:**
- it-c: Tuscany, Umbria, Marche, Lazio, Abruzzo, Molise
- it-s: Campania, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria, Sicily, Sardinia

**Official register:** MASAF's national register of denominations,
Federdoc, and eAmbrosia. Count DOCG, DOC and IGT per region against them.
The existing convention is `classification: DOCG` / `DOC` / `IGT`; the
macro regions have none.

**`grapes:` field, yes, for every DOCG/DOC:** the principal varieties its
disciplinare names. For example, Barolo: Nebbiolo; Amarone: Corvina,
Corvinone, Rondinella; Soave: Garganega; Franciacorta: Chardonnay, Pinot
Nero, Pinot Bianco. Skip IGTs unless the disciplinare is
variety-specific.

**Grapes file:** northern Italian natives and local names missing from
`grapes.yaml`. Check:
- Piedmont: Timorasso, Ruché, Grignolino, Freisa, Pelaverga, Nascetta,
  Erbaluce, Vespolina, Croatina, Uva Rara.
- Veneto and Lombardy: Corvinone, Oseleta, Durella, Chiavennasca (=
  Nebbiolo), Groppello.
- Trentino-Alto Adige: Marzemino, Nosiola, Teroldego, Lagrein, Schiava /
  Vernatsch, Pinot Bianco / Weissburgunder.
- Friuli: Friulano / Tocai Friulano, Ribolla Gialla, Picolit, Verduzzo,
  Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso, Schioppettino, Pignolo, Tazzelenghe,
  Vitovska, Terrano.
- Emilia-Romagna: the Lambruscos (distinct varieties per VIVC), Pignoletto
  / Grechetto Gentile, Albana, Fortana.

This slice is large.
